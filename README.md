## 📐 My Architect-First Engineering Workflow

I believe good software engineering starts before writing code.

Understanding the business requirements, identifying constraints and designing the right solution are just as important as implementation.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A["Business Requirements"] --> B["Data & Metrics"]
    B --> C["Architecture & Design"]
    C --> D["Implementation"]
    D --> E["Testing & Quality"]
    E --> F["Deployment"]
    F --> G["Monitoring & Optimization"]

    style A fill:#0077B5,color:#fff
    style B fill:#0077B5,color:#fff
    style C fill:#0077B5,color:#fff
    style D fill:#0077B5,color:#fff
    style E fill:#0077B5,color:#fff
    style F fill:#0077B5,color:#fff
    style G fill:#0077B5,color:#fff
```

My development principles:

- Understand the problem before choosing the technology.
- Design for maintainability and scalability.
- Build incrementally and validate assumptions.
- Automate repetitive processes.
- Treat testing and quality as part of development.
- Measure outcomes rather than just counting features.
